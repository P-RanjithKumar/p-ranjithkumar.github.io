AURA

AURA is a Windows desktop app for wedding photographers and event organisers. It runs on your own PC and sorts event photos by recognising faces. Guests then use a simple web page on their phone to find their own photos by taking a selfie.

How it works

  1. Add your event photos. AURA runs on your own PC and recognises the faces in your photos there. Face data is not sent to us or anyone else.
  2. AURA sorts and uploads them. With your permission, it uploads the sorted photos to your own Google Drive and shares them.
  3. Guests find their photos. Each guest opens a web page on their phone, takes a selfie and finds the photos they are in.
