Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

AURA is a Windows desktop app made by DARK Intelligence for wedding photographers and organisers. In short: AURA runs on your own PC, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

What AURA accesses

  • Your event photos. AURA works on photos you choose to add, on your own PC.
  • Faces in those photos. AURA recognises faces to sort photos by person. This face recognition runs on your own PC. Face data is not sent to us or to any third party.
  • Your Google Drive (limited). AURA asks for Google Drive permission using only this scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This gives AURA access only to files and folders that AURA itself creates in your own Google Drive. It cannot see your other files.

How it is used

AURA uses the Google Drive permission only to upload the sorted photos to your Google Drive and to share them. Guests use a web page on their phone to find their photos by taking a selfie. We do not use your information for anything else.

Where it is stored

AURA runs on your own PC. Your Google sign-in tokens are stored only on your own PC. We never receive, see or store them, and we run no server that holds user data. The sorted photos are stored in your own Google Drive.

Nothing is shared

The developer does not collect, sell or share personal data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AURA's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Revoke Google access any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete data on your PC by deleting AURA's data folder.
